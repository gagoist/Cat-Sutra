품: 무량의경 · 설법품 제이(無量義經 說法品 第二)
품영: Sutra of Innumerable Meanings · Preaching, Chapter 2

----- 원문 -----
善男子 我先道場 菩提樹下 端坐六年 得成阿耨多羅三藐三菩提
선남자 아선도장 보리수하 단좌육년 득성아뇩다라삼먁삼보리
以佛眼觀 一切諸法 不可宣説 所以者何
이불안관 일체제법 불가선설 소이자하
知諸衆生 性欲不同 性欲不同 種種説法 以方便力 四十餘年 未顯真実
지제중생 성욕부동 성욕부동 종종설법 이방편력 사십여년 미현진실

----- 해설 -----
설법품에서 가장 무거운 한 구절입니다.
깨달은 뒤에도 사십여 년 ‘아직 진실을 나타내지 않았다’는 고백입니다.
법화경 방편품이 말하는 방편과 진실의 문이, 여기서 먼저 열립니다.
도를 얻음에 차별이 있던 까닭은 법이 여러 갈래여서가 아니라, 근기에 맞춘 방편이 진실을 가리고 있었기 때문입니다.

----- 영문 -----
Good sons, I myself sat upright for six years beneath the bodhi tree in the place of the Way, and accomplished anuttarā-samyak-saṃbodhi.
With the Buddha-eye I observed all dharmas, yet they could not be declared.
Why?
Because I knew that the natures and desires of living beings were not the same.
Because natures and desires were not the same, I preached the Dharma in many ways.
I preached in many ways by the power of expedient means, and for more than forty years I had not yet revealed the true reality.
For this reason living beings differ in attaining the Way, and cannot swiftly accomplish unsurpassed bodhi.

----- 영문해설 -----
This is the heaviest line in the chapter of preaching.
It is the confession that even after awakening, for more than forty years, “the true reality had not yet been revealed.”
The gate of expedient means and the true, which the Lotus Sutra’s chapter on expedient means will speak, opens here first.
Beings differed in attaining the Way not because the Dharma had many branches, but because expedients fitted to faculty still veiled the true.
